First-season archive / the robot

Mecanum wheels. An extending arm. Our own claw.

Our first-season robot brought these mechanisms together. This page records that build, rather than the current season’s robot.

01 / MECHANISMS

What we built.

01

Mecanum drivetrain

We switched to mecanum wheels in November of our first season so the robot could move in different directions.

02

Arm with a Viper slide

We added the Viper slide in December to extend the arm. Testing brought attention to how reach, weight, and balance affected the robot.

03

The Cyber Claw

Our custom 3D-printed claw was designed to pick up samples and specimens. It was one of the mechanisms we developed ourselves.

02 / A PRACTICAL FIX

We tried hitches as counterweights.

The arm made balance a real design problem. Working within our budget, we explored inexpensive options and repurposed hitches to adjust the robot’s weight distribution.

Our portfolio also identified limiting the Viper arm’s extension and reducing the robot’s width as possible improvements.
