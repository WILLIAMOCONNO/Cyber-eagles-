Outreach / Rock Ridge High School

Bring the robot. Start a conversation.

At the FIRST Expo at Rock Ridge High School, we showed children and parents what our team had been working on.

AT THE EXPO

More useful when you can see it move.

01 / THE DISPLAY

Introduce the team.

We set up a booth with a tri-fold display explaining our team and robot.

02 / THE DEMONSTRATION

Show the mechanisms.

We demonstrated the robot and explained how its mechanisms worked through interactive demonstrations.

03 / THE QUESTIONS

Keep the conversation going.

Children asked questions about the robot. Parents wanted to know how to start an FTC club.

WHAT WE HEARD

Could you teach us block programming?

Some visitors asked us to teach block programming in a future session. Their interest gave us an idea for a YouTube channel where we could share what we were learning.

The expo also gave us practice explaining our work to people who had never seen an FTC robot before.

The channel and future teaching sessions were ideas recorded in our portfolio. No channel or upcoming event is announced here.

BEYOND THE BOOTH

Learning how to run a team, too.

Alongside building and outreach, we researched equipment costs and worked on a budget. Those decisions helped us connect our engineering plans to the resources available.
