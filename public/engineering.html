01 / WORKING TOGETHER

The mechanism and the code have to agree.

Building a robot means making mechanical and programming decisions together. The arm needs to reach its target; the robot needs to stay balanced; the program needs to produce a repeatable movement.

Our first-season portfolio records a switch to mecanum wheels, a Viper slide for more reach, and a custom 3D-printed Cyber Claw. Each change gave us something else to test.
