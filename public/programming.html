THE TOOLS

Blocks, encoders, and telemetry.

We practiced with Blockly before writing our first FTC OpModes. Our autonomous routines used Blocks programming and encoder feedback for movement.

We set positions for the neck and Cyber Claw, then used telemetry to help fine-tune behavior. Alongside autonomous, we wrote a basic TeleOp program for driver control.

OPEN QUESTIONS

What we wanted to explore next.

Our portfolio identified three possible improvements: odometry, AprilTag reading, and a color sensor.

These were development ideas. The portfolio did not claim they were completed features.

These programs belong to our first-season archive. Their zone names and objectives refer to that season’s game.

BEYOND THE PROGRAM

Code meets the actual robot.

Our programming work happened alongside changes to the drivetrain, arm, and claw. Testing gave us a way to compare what we wanted the robot to do with what it actually did.
