The people behind team 27662

We started our own team.

A school robotics waitlist gave us a reason to build something of our own. That became the Cyber Eagles.

01 / OUR BEGINNING

A place to learn every part of robotics.

When we were waitlisted for a school robotics team, we decided to start a community team in Ashburn, Virginia. Most of us were new to robotics.

We wanted to learn the whole job: choosing components, designing mechanisms, building, programming, and testing. Along the way, we also learned how to make decisions together and ask for help.

02 / THE TEAM

Bringing their own ideas to the same design, programming, and testing challenges.

We are a FIRST Tech Challenge team with a mix of experience levels. That means explaining our ideas, listening to each other, and learning new skills as the robot develops.

Our first-year Innovation Award was an encouraging start. The work since then has kept giving us new problems to solve.

03 / HOW WE WORK

Everyone has something to figure out.

A mechanism may need another revision. A program may need more testing. We share ideas, try alternatives, and use what happened to decide what to change.

The Cyber Claw, our arm extension, and our balancing experiments are some of the results of that approach.
