Write down what needs work.

Our first-season SWOT assessment named the good parts of the robot and the problems we still needed to solve.

THE ASSESSMENT

What the portfolio recorded.

01 / STRENGTHS

A team ready to learn. A claw of our own.

Enthusiastic teammates, a shared interest in robotics, and our custom 3D-printed Cyber Claw.

02 / WEAKNESSES

Width and weight distribution.

A wider robot body and challenges with balance and weight distribution.

03 / OPPORTUNITIES

Make specific changes.

Improve balance, limit Viper arm extension, reduce the robot’s width, and expand outreach and STEM education.

04 / THREATS

Protect the electronics.

Electrostatic discharge (ESD), interference, and the risk of servos burning out.

THE NEXT ITERATION

Turn an observation into a change.

The assessment gave us a starting point for the next round of brainstorming, building, programming, and testing. “Improve balance” is more useful when it leads to a specific change we can try.

These notes describe our first-season robot. They are an archive of what we learned, rather than an assessment of the current build.
